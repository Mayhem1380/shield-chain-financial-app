Beginner's Handbook

What is crypto?

Digital assets can move over blockchain networks. Their prices can change sharply, and losses are possible.

How trading works

Buying and selling assets can generate gains or losses. Fees and volatility matter; profits are never guaranteed.

Protect your keys

Never share recovery phrases, private keys, verification codes or passwords with anyone.

Security first

Know the warning signs

Beware of urgent messages, fake support staff, suspicious links, guaranteed returns and requests to send crypto to unlock withdrawals. Verify website addresses independently and enable multifactor authentication where available.

Never trust an unsolicited message claiming to be ShieldChain Financial support.

Our mission

Built around clarity and scam awareness

ShieldChain Financial aims to make digital-asset education and platform navigation easier for beginners and experienced users alike. Our mission is to put transparent information, practical warnings and understandable tools first.
