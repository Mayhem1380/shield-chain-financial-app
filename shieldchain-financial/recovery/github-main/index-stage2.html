Learn before you act

Beginner's Handbook

1. What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets that may use blockchain networks. They can be volatile and may lose substantial value.

2. How do trades work?

People buy and sell at changing market prices. Trading can produce losses as well as gains, and fees reduce results.

3. Custodial vs self-custody

With custodial services a provider holds assets or keys. With self-custody, you manage your keys and recovery information yourself.

4. Protect your recovery phrase

Never type your seed phrase into a website sent through a message, and never share it with support staff.

5. Understand the risks

Price volatility, platform failures, fraud, cyberattacks and regulatory changes can affect digital assets.

6. Check before sending

Crypto transfers can be irreversible. Verify the destination and network independently before making any real transfer elsewhere.

Awareness is protection

Security Centre

Common scam warning signs

Unexpected messages, urgent account warnings, fake customer support, requests for recovery phrases, promises of guaranteed returns and demands to pay fees to unlock withdrawals are all red flags.

Use independently verified website addresses. Enable multifactor authentication where supported. Do not trust screenshots or unsolicited messages as proof of legitimacy.
