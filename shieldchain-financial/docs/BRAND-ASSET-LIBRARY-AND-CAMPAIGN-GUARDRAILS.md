# ShieldChain Financial — visual asset library and publication rules

## Purpose

The `public/assets/` directory contains compressed WebP concept artwork for the SF brand. The public homepage uses a small, curated subset in the **The SF Brand World** gallery. Artwork is illustrative and must not be mistaken for proof of operational capability, licensing, security certification, partnerships, market performance or customer outcomes.

## Copy and compliance guardrails

- Do not use “become a millionaire”, “make profits”, “invest safely”, “secure your assets” or similar phrases as guaranteed outcomes or factual claims without substantiation and qualified review.
- Never promise returns, capital protection, risk-free investing, or guaranteed profits. Crypto and other investments can lose value.
- The current homepage gallery explicitly labels its imagery as concept art and describes the limitations.
- Prize promotions require separate written approval and current terms before launch. The supplied giveaway artwork is a draft, not an active offer.
- Check token names/logos, trademarks, campaign permissions, accessibility, image rights and any jurisdiction-specific financial-promotion rules before public campaigns.
- Keep originals supplied by the founder in the conversation upload; the project contains optimized WebP derivatives for faster web delivery.

## Homepage implementation

The homepage includes four curated images with descriptive alternative text, lazy loading, responsive layouts and a visible concept-art disclaimer. Remaining supplied assets are retained in `public/assets/` for future reviewed use. Publicly served assets are not private; do not place confidential or licensed-for-internal-use-only material in this directory.
