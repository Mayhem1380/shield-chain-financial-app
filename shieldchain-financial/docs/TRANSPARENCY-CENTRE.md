# Transparency Centre

Route: `/transparency`

The page gives prospective beta users a concise explanation of the SF concept's current state, digital-asset risks, expected fee disclosure principles and launch gates.

## Accuracy rules
- Market values on the demo dashboard are illustrative, not live quotes.
- Do not present a fee schedule as approved until it is reviewed and implemented.
- Do not imply that custody, authentication, identity checks, payments, trading, withdrawals, payouts, licences or certifications are connected or verified unless evidence is available.
- General risk language is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific product disclosures or legal review.
- Revisit this page whenever the actual product scope, providers, fees or regulatory status changes.

The route is informational only. It does not connect to providers, collect identity documents, initiate orders, or enable financial operations.
