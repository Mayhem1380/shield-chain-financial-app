Beginner's Handbook

What is cryptocurrency?

A digital asset that may use a blockchain network. Its value can change quickly, and losses can be substantial.

How do trades work?

People buy and sell at changing prices. A price increase may create a gain; a price decrease may create a loss.

What is self-custody?

You control your wallet's keys. Losing a recovery phrase can mean losing access permanently; never share it.

What is a demo account?

A practice environment using fictional values. Results do not predict real investment outcomes.

Security first

Security Centre

Protect your accounts

Use unique passwords, multifactor authentication and trusted official websites.

Spot phishing

Be cautious with unexpected links, attachments, fake support messages and urgent requests for payment.

Protect your recovery phrase

Never type your wallet recovery phrase into a link sent by email, social media or chat.

Security education is not a guarantee against fraud, theft or financial loss.
