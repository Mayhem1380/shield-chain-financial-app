# ShieldChain revenue and pricing principles

This document is a planning framework, not an approved fee schedule or financial forecast.

## Revenue streams to evaluate
- Exchange/swap service fee, where legally permitted and supported by a contracted provider.
- Clearly priced prop-trading challenge entry, only after rules, consumer disclosures, eligibility and payout operations are reviewed.
- Optional education or professional tools, with transparent recurring pricing and simple cancellation.
- Business/API services only after demand, unit economics and regulatory scope are validated.

## Pricing rules
1. Show the total cost before a customer confirms: platform fee, provider/liquidity cost, network/route fee, spread/slippage assumptions, quote expiry and estimated receive amount.
2. Do not label a fee "best", "lowest" or "industry-leading" without current, comparable evidence.
3. The 0.25%, 0.35% and 0.50% values in the swap calculator are examples only. No rate is approved.
4. Model contribution margin per transaction: revenue less provider fees, liquidity costs, network costs absorbed by the platform, payment processing, fraud/chargebacks, support and compliance allocation.
5. Include sensitivity cases for low volume, high volatility, provider outages, failed/returned transactions, refunds, chargebacks and fraud.
6. Never depend on customer losses, hidden charges, withdrawal friction, retroactive rule changes or misleading marketing to make the business profitable.
7. Challenge fees and reward obligations need a separate model that stress-tests payout rates, abuse, operational costs and fair treatment. Never imply simulated results guarantee live rewards.
8. Do not project profitability until supplier quotes, acquisition costs, staffing, legal/compliance costs and realistic customer cohorts are evidenced.

## Launch gates
- Provider contracts and service-level commitments reviewed.
- Regulatory perimeter and target-country availability signed off by qualified counsel.
- Pricing and disclosures tested for comprehension.
- Reconciliation, refunds, complaints and incident handling tested end-to-end.
- Financial model reviewed independently and runway/reserves assessed.
